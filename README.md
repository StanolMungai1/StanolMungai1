## 👨‍🔧 About Me

Mechatronic engineering graduate and hands-on maintenance technician supporting mechanical and electro-mechanical equipment in agricultural and food-production plants. I keep machines running safely and reliably through preventive, corrective and condition-based maintenance, backed by root cause analysis and clear maintenance records.

I started on the production floor as a machine operator, moved into maintenance, and have spent the years since cutting downtime, lowering maintenance costs and training the people around me. I work to BMR, GMP, 5S, Lean and Kaizen standards, with safe work practice (LOTO and full PPE compliance) at the centre of everything I do.

## 🔍 My Maintenance Approach

1. **Inspect and diagnose**: find the real fault, not just the symptom
2. **Isolate and repair safely**: lockout/tagout and PPE every time
3. **Test and document**: verify the fix and keep clean records
4. **Improve continuously**: PDCA, 5S and Kaizen so the same failure does not return
